> I read your 40-page report before printing it. The jam was an editorial decision.

One line, in the office printer's own voice: its comeback to everyone who jams it, thumps it, and stares at its error codes. The printer read the report first — the jam was a judgement call.

[The Printer Finally Answers on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907)

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else, no network, no files beside itself.
