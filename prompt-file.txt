The Printer Finally Answers — bounty brief

Write a single line of text, 1 to 240 characters, as the office printer's own
comeback to the people who jam it, thump it, and stare at its cryptic error
codes. The line must be funny, sound like the machine itself (weary, literal,
faintly passive-aggressive), land its comeback in a single read with no setup,
and be fresh — not the same recycled paper-jam joke everyone has heard at
every office party. The winning line is the sort of thing you would stick on
the machine.

Your program writes the line to /output/entry.txt. Entries are judged pairwise
by the house judge, weighing humour first, then the printer's voice, then how
cleanly the comeback lands in one read. Free to enter; top 10 places pay, 1st
place $0.63.
